Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Introduction

Polar Bear Detailing ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website polarbeardetailing (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our Site or use our services.

We are committed to keeping your data safe. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information We Collect

When you fill out the contact form on our Site, you may voluntarily provide the following information:

  • Name — to address you properly
  • Phone number — so we can respond to your inquiry
  • Service selection — the detailing package you're interested in
  • Message — any additional details about your vehicle or request

How Your Information Is Processed

Our contact form does not send your data to any server, database, or third-party service. When you click "Send Request," the form simply opens your device's default SMS (text messaging) application with a pre-filled message to our business phone number.

This means:

  • Your information is not stored on our website or any server
  • Your information is not transmitted over the internet through our Site
  • The text message is sent directly from your phone to ours, just like any other SMS conversation
  • We only receive your information if you choose to send the text message

Cookies & Tracking

Our Site does not use:

  • Cookies
  • Analytics services (such as Google Analytics)
  • Advertising pixels or trackers
  • Any form of behavioral tracking

Our Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect basic server logs (such as IP addresses) as part of standard web hosting. This is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party services, such as our Instagram page. These external sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices. We encourage you to review their policies before providing any personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Since we do not store your personal data on any server or database, there is no data to request, modify, or delete. If you have texted us and would like us to delete the SMS conversation from our phone, please let us know and we will comply.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
